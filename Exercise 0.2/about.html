About Us

Project Purpose

This website was created as part of COS30045 - Data Visualisation at Swinburne University of Technology. The purpose is to demonstrate foundational web development skills including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while exploring the topic of appliance energy consumption in Australian households.

The project serves as a practical exercise to prepare for more advanced data visualisation tasks using libraries like D3.js in later weeks of the semester.

Our Vision

To empower Australian households with knowledge about appliance energy consumption, enabling smarter, more sustainable choices that reduce both environmental impact and household costs.

Our Mission

To provide clear, accessible, and interactive data visualisations that help users understand their energy usage patterns, make informed decisions, and contribute to a more sustainable future through conscious energy consumption.
