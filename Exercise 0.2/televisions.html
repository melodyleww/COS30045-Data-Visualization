Television Energy Consumption

Understanding the energy impact of TVs in Australian homes

TV Energy Usage

Televisions are a common appliance in Australian households, with many homes having multiple TVs. Modern televisions, especially LED and OLED models, are significantly more energy-efficient than older CRT or plasma models.

A typical modern LED TV consumes between 50-150 watts depending on screen size and technology. Larger screens and OLED technology generally consume more energy.

Energy Comparison by TV Type

  • LED/LCD (32 inches): ~50-70 watts
  • LED/LCD (55 inches): ~80-120 watts
  • OLED (55 inches): ~100-150 watts
  • Plasma (older): ~200-300 watts
